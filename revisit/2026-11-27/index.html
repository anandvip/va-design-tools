From ideation to practice

April 2025

A place to collect image styles for AI image tools, keep a project's image treatment consistent, and gather favourites, with numerology and maths as lenses on design.

November 2026

The repo becomes the home of a written design language. Swiss Ledger now has named tokens, type rules and components, and it already runs a live page, the VedicVA timing report.

The question has moved from "which styles do I like?" to "which rules can a team of AI agents build from, the same way every time?" Collecting favourites was the first step toward writing a system down.

Added with this revisit

Swiss Ledger design guidelines: zero fluff, high signal, pure CSS and offline. Chosen from three first-round drafts.

A note to my future self

The time spent here in 2025 was not wasted. Sorting styles, testing palettes and reading patterns into numbers was your mind working ahead, in timelines that hadn't arrived yet.

Those early pages taught you what you like and why. That is exactly what made it possible to pick one design language out of three and write it down clearly. Keep returning here. Each visit builds on the last.
